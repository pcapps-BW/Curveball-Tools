Card Alarms — Fleet Equipment Sweep

Reads show ne alarms on every NPT-1250 and reports the EQUIPMENT/hardware alarms — card, fan, power, MCP, temperature — grouped by market, with new-vs-known tracking so a freshly-failed card stands out from one already known. Leave all checked for everything, or narrow to just what you're after (e.g. card alarms only). The sweep always reads every device; this only filters the report.
